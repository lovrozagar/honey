bench/hotpath-baseline.json. Update only with `--update` and commit the move.

== v1 rule ==
A v1 run may not regress the hot-path ratio beyond +25% per scenario. A
regression fails the bit. Baselines are per-runtime-minor-version; the one
that moved is re-recorded with `--update` in the same change that moved it,
with a one-line reason. The split of the Honey god-class into pure decision
functions is expected to hold or improve the ratios (v0 already removed the
per-request middleware allocations by compiling chains at finalize).
